# Product Overview

Vlixxo is an ecommerce storefront focused on simple product discovery and shopping across supported markets.

## Public customer experience

The live product currently exposes public shopping surfaces for:

- Home and merchandising
- Shop/catalogue browsing
- Categories
- Product search
- Visual product search
- Product detail pages
- Cart
- Checkout handoff
- Shipping, returns and customer policies
- Responsive mobile use

## Discoverability

Vlixxo includes search-engine-oriented product metadata and public discovery surfaces. The implementation also contains mechanisms for keeping canonical URLs and catalogue discovery aligned with the live storefront.

## Scope of this repository

This repository documents the product without publishing the private production implementation, credentials or operational data.
